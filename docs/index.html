The problem

Meetings are where decisions happen — and where they get lost.

You can either be present or take good notes — not both. So context slips, action items evaporate, and the real decision lives in someone's memory until the next "wait, what did we agree on?"

  • ✕ Half your attention goes to scribbling instead of the conversation.
  • ✕ Generic transcription tools hand you a wall of text — not answers.
  • ✕ Decisions and owners get buried; follow-through quietly dies.
  • ✕ Last month's meetings are impossible to search when you need them.
The solution

One always-on brain for every conversation.

Meeting Copilot listens, understands, and remembers — so you can stay in the room. It captures the transcript, answers questions as the meeting happens, and turns the recording into structured, searchable knowledge.

  • ✓ Be fully present — the copilot handles the record-keeping.
  • ✓ Ask "what did we decide?" and get a grounded answer, live.
  • ✓ Every decision, risk, and action item extracted automatically.
  • ✓ Search across all your meetings by meaning, not keywords.
Features

Everything you need, before and after the call

A complete meeting workflow — capture, understand, act, and recall — in one polished app.

Real-time transcription

Deepgram Nova-2 streams a live, accurate transcript with speaker diarization — captured from mic and system audio, saved as you speak.

Live AI copilot

Ask anything mid-meeting — "who owns this?", "summarize the last 5 minutes" — and get RAG-grounded answers streamed token-by-token, with citations to the moment.

Summaries & action items

The instant you stop, Groq generates an executive summary, key decisions, risks/blockers, and prioritized action items with owners and due dates.

Semantic search

Find any moment or past meeting by meaning, not exact words — powered by Gemini embeddings and an on-device SQLite vector store.

Google Calendar integration

Connect your calendar and upcoming meetings appear automatically — one click to start recording or open a prep brief, so you're never scrambling before a call.

Know the room before you join

Pre-meeting briefs research the participants and surface their background, shared context, and suggested talking points — so you walk in prepared for everyone at the table.

Share & export anywhere

One-click export to Markdown, PDF, or audio. Send recaps to email and Slack, copy a shareable link, and stay in sync with your calendar.

Web & native desktop

Use it in the browser, or install the Electron desktop app for system-audio capture and a floating widget that stays with you across every call.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Which platforms does it run on?+
Both a web app (any modern browser) and a native desktop app built with Electron for macOS, Windows, and Linux. The desktop build adds system-audio capture and a floating widget that stays on top during calls.
Does the AI really work during the meeting?+
Yes. The transcript is saved live, and the AI copilot answers questions in real time using retrieval-augmented generation over what's been said so far — streamed token-by-token via Server-Sent Events. It also works after the meeting.
What AI models power it?+
Deepgram Nova-2 for streaming speech-to-text with diarization, Groq-hosted LLMs for summaries and chat, and Google Gemini embeddings for semantic search. You bring your own API keys.
How is my data handled?+
Meeting Copilot uses an embedded SQLite database for both relational data and the vector store — no Postgres and no separate vector database. It runs on your own credentials, keeping you in control of your data.
Can I export and share recaps?+
Absolutely. Export any meeting to Markdown, PDF, or audio, copy a shareable link, or send the recap straight to Slack and email. Summaries, transcripts, and notes are all included.
Do I need to set up a database server?+
No. SQLite is created on disk automatically — there's nothing to provision or run. Getting started is just Node, pnpm, and your API keys.
